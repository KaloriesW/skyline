SKYLINE / TERRAIN WATCH — TWO-MINUTE SUBMISSION DEMO

Target: 1:50–1:58, including short click pauses. Do not exceed 2:00.
Read only NARRATION. ACTION describes the screen, not words to read aloud.
Use a real completed scan from History. Its acquisition date and measured results must remain visible. Do not imply reviewing history performs new inference.

00:00–00:15 | THE PROBLEM
ACTION: Show the Skyline home workspace, then open a real completed scan from History.
NARRATION:
After heavy rain in Taiwan, where should a reviewer look first? Skyline Terrain Watch turns free satellite imagery into a map of suspected landslides, with the evidence needed to inspect them.

00:15–00:32 | THE MODEL
ACTION: Show the orange outlines. Briefly show the U-Net model name in Settings.
NARRATION:
Our trained U-Net uses eleven Sentinel-2 bands, elevation and slope. It segments candidate areas, then adds vegetation change, nearby roads and weather. These outlines come from the model, not hand-drawn shapes.

00:32–00:50 | BEFORE AND AFTER
ACTION: Open Before / after. Move the comparison slider, then close comparison.
NARRATION:
This is a real completed scan from our local history. Its acquisition date, footprint and candidate count are visible here. The before-and-after view helps a reviewer compare vegetation and exposed ground. Clouds and missing data remain unassessed.

00:50–01:09 | PRIORITIZE AND LOCATE
ACTION: In Review filters, select roads within 200 m; then minimum footprint 1 ha.
Show the resulting candidates, open one, and point to its coordinates/export.
NARRATION:
We can filter candidates by proximity to roads and by footprint size to focus the review. Each has coordinates and exportable geometry. Nearby does not mean the road is blocked.

01:09–01:26 | QWEN AND MONITORING
ACTION: Show a preloaded, real Qwen response about the completed scan. Return home and
show the monitoring controls, left OFF. Do not start a new download during filming.
NARRATION:
Qwen explains scans and answers follow-up questions using weather, air-quality and model results. A user can also preview an area and enable hourly checks for newly published satellite images. New captures are days apart, and the server must stay running.

01:26–01:45 | MEASURED RESULTS
ACTION: Show README Results or the evaluation evidence. Keep the scope visible.
NARRATION:
Our retained model reached fifty-four-point-three percent pixel IoU and seventy-point-four percent Dice on twenty-four inventory-labelled patches. That small holdout was repeatedly compared during development. Live-image accuracy still needs independent labels, and these are suspected regions, not confirmed landslides.

01:45–01:57 | WHY IT MATTERS
ACTION: Return to the segmented map; finish on one candidate and its evidence.
NARRATION:
We built this to help reviewers prioritize inspection with dated, inspectable evidence. Our next step is independent review with local experts. Terrain Watch supports human decisions; it does not predict when a slope will fail.
